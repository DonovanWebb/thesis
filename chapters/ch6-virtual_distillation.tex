\chapter{Virtual Distillation on an Ion Trap QC}
\label{ch:virtual_distillation}
\minitoc

This chapter presents the theory of virtual distillation, a purification based error mitigation technique. We begin with the background and uses of error mitigation, and detail the virtual distillation protocol. Following this, we analyse the performance of virtual distillation under different physically motivated noise models, with 
experimental results presented for two-copies in the presence of both spatially correlated and uncorrelated noise. We close this chapter with a discussion on extending this method to more than two-copies, and to hybrid systems.
